# Day 19 – Build a Football Intelligence Hub with Claude AI

## Objective

The objective of Day 19 was to build an AI-powered Football Intelligence Experience using Claude AI.

The goal was to analyze football knowledge, create a FIFA World Cup 2026 prediction report, evaluate football awareness, and generate a personality-based football profile.

---

# Workflow

## Tool Used

- Claude AI

## Approach

Since I am a beginner in football, the analysis was generated with beginner-friendly explanations and focused on learning rather than complex football terminology.
