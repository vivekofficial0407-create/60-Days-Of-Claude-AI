# Day 3 - Role-Based Prompting

## Challenge Objective

The objective of this challenge was to understand how assigning different roles to Claude affects the quality, depth, and perspective of AI-generated responses.

---

## What is Role-Based Prompting?

Role-Based Prompting is a Prompt Engineering technique where we instruct AI to act as a specific person or expert before asking a question.

Instead of asking a generic question, we assign a role such as:

* Founder
* Software Developer
* Product Manager
* HR Manager
* Marketer

This helps the AI generate responses tailored to that specific perspective.

---

## Question Used

How can I build a successful AI-powered startup in India in 2026?

---

# Prompt 1: Without Role

## Prompt

How can I build a successful AI-powered startup in India in 2026?

## Output Summary

Claude provided a broad and research-based response covering:

* AI market opportunities
* Government initiatives
* Startup funding
* Industry trends
* AI growth potential in India

### Observation

The answer was informative and detailed but remained general in nature.

---

# Prompt 2: Founder Persona

## Prompt

You are a successful startup founder who has built and scaled multiple AI startups.

How can I build a successful AI-powered startup in India in 2026?

## Output Summary

Claude focused on:

* Startup opportunities
* Product-market fit
* Distribution strategy
* Fundraising
* Building competitive advantages
* Scaling a startup

### Observation

The response became practical, business-oriented, and execution-focused.

---

# Prompt 3: Developer Persona

## Prompt

You are a senior AI software engineer with experience building production AI applications.

How can I build a successful AI-powered startup in India in 2026?

## Output Summary

Claude focused on:

* AI architecture
* Data pipelines
* Model selection
* Infrastructure
* Evaluation systems
* Production deployment

### Observation

The response became highly technical and implementation-focused.

---

# Comparison

| Prompt Type       | Response Style                       |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Without Role      | General and research-based           |
| Founder Persona   | Business and growth-focused          |
| Developer Persona | Technical and implementation-focused |

---

# Key Learnings

1. The same question can generate completely different responses when different roles are assigned.

2. Role-Based Prompting helps AI provide more relevant and context-aware answers.

3. Founder prompts focus on business opportunities and growth.

4. Developer prompts focus on implementation and technical execution.

5. Assigning a role transforms AI from a general assistant into a specialized expert.

6. Better prompts lead to better outputs.
