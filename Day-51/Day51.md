# 🚀Day 51 - Product Discovery & Sprint Planning
## ABTalks 60 Days Claude AI Challenge

## 📌Overview

Day 51 marked the beginning of the 10-day capstone project. Instead of jumping directly into coding, today's focus was on product discovery, requirement gathering, project planning, and defining a realistic MVP.

After evaluating multiple AI product ideas, I finalized **InterviewPilot AI**, an AI-powered interview preparation platform that combines resume analysis, personalized interview question generation, mock interviews, AI feedback, and interview readiness reporting into one streamlined workflow.

The objective was to establish a strong foundation that will guide the remaining nine days of development.

# 💡Final Product

## InterviewPilot AI

InterviewPilot AI is a full-stack AI-powered interview preparation platform that helps candidates prepare for interviews through one focused workflow.

The application enables users to:

- Upload a PDF resume
- Extract and review resume content
- Select a target job role
- Generate AI-powered personalized interview questions
- Complete a mock interview
- Receive detailed AI feedback
- View an Interview Readiness Report
- Export the final report
