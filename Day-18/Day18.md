# Day 18 – Build a Brain Dump Action Planner Skill

## Objective

The goal of Day 18 was to create an AI-powered Brain Dump Action Planner that transforms unstructured notes, meeting discussions, brainstorming sessions, and project conversations into a structured and actionable dashboard.

Instead of manually organizing information, AI automatically extracts:

* Key takeaways
* Action items
* Decisions
* Open questions
* Risks and blockers
* Pending issues

---

## Problem Statement

Meetings and brainstorming sessions often generate large amounts of information that become difficult to track.

Important tasks, ownership details, deadlines, and decisions can easily get lost inside long notes.

This project demonstrates how AI can convert messy information into a structured project management dashboard.
