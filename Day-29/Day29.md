# Day 29 - Build Operation Lifeline: Supply Chain Crisis Lab

## Project Overview

As part of the 60 Days of Claude AI Challenge, I built an interactive business simulation game using Claude AI.

The project is called:

# Operation Lifeline: Supply Chain Crisis Lab


It simulates how business leaders respond to real-world supply chain disruptions.

The user plays the role of a CEO and makes strategic decisions during a supply chain crisis.


---

# Objective

The goal of this simulator is to understand:

- Supply chain risk management
- Crisis response planning
- Supplier negotiation
- Leadership decision making
- AI-powered business optimization


---

# Technology Used

- React
- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript
- Babel CDN
- Claude AI


The application works as a single-file offline HTML application.


---

# Core Features


## 1. Random Company Generation

Every simulation creates a fictional company with:

- Industry
- Revenue
- Factories
- Warehouses
- Suppliers
- Inventory levels
- Lead time
- Operating countries


This creates a unique business environment every time.


---

# 2. Supply Chain Crisis Generator

The simulator randomly generates different crisis scenarios:

Examples:

- Factory Fire
- Supplier Bankruptcy
- Port Strike
- Cyberattack
- Flood
- Raw Material Shortage
- Political Conflict
- Shipping Delay


Each crisis includes:

- Business impact
- Urgency
- Operational challenges


---

# 3. War Room Decision System

The CEO enters a crisis management room.

The player selects 3 response actions from multiple options.


Decisions impact:

- Cost
- Inventory
- Profit
- Delivery Speed
- Customer Satisfaction


The simulator visually updates business performance.


---

# 4. Supplier Negotiation Simulation

The project includes multiple negotiation rounds.

Each decision affects:

- Supplier Trust
- Price
- Lead Time


The goal is balancing:

Short-term savings vs long-term relationships.


---

# 5. CEO Leadership Boardroom

The simulator tests executive decision making through scenario-based questions.

Leadership areas:

- Crisis communication
- Cost management
- Strategic planning
- Customer retention
- Supplier management


---

# 6. AI Strategy Investment

The CEO can invest in AI solutions.

Available options:

- Demand Forecasting AI
- Inventory Optimization
- Supplier Risk Monitoring
- Warehouse Vision
- Procurement Copilot


The AI investment decisions influence future resilience.


---

# 7. Final Executive Dashboard

The final dashboard displays:

## Overall Crisis Score

0-100


## Business Metrics

- Leadership Score

- Cost Control
