# Day 8 – Personal Environmental Health Analyzer

## Objective

Build an interactive environmental health dashboard using Claude AI to analyze air quality, water quality, environmental health scores, and personalized recommendations.

## Tools Used

* Claude AI (Sonnet)
* HTML
* Interactive Dashboard Components
* Data Visualization
* AQI & Water Quality Analysis

## Project Overview

I used Claude AI to generate a fully interactive Environmental Health Analyzer dashboard. The application analyzes environmental conditions across major Indian cities and provides insights related to air quality, water quality, health risks, and environmental scores.

The dashboard includes:

* AQI comparison charts
* PM2.5 comparison charts
* PM10 comparison charts
* City ranking visualizations
* AQI distribution analysis
* Interactive filters
* Environmental Health Score
* Personalized recommendations
* Insights and anomaly detection
