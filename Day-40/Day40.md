# Day 40 - CareerPrep AI | Resume & Interview Readiness Coach

## 🚀 Project Overview

**CareerPrep AI** is an AI-powered Resume & Interview Readiness Assistant developed as part of the **60 Days of Claude AI Challenge**.

The application helps students and job seekers evaluate their resumes through an AI-driven analysis workflow. Users can upload or paste their resume, optionally provide a target job description, and receive an estimated ATS score, structured feedback, interview readiness insights, and actionable recommendations.

Unlike a traditional chatbot, the application provides a purpose-built interface focused on resume evaluation, making the experience more intuitive, practical, and recruiter-friendly.

# 🧠 AI Assistant Architecture

The assistant is designed around a production-style system prompt that defines:

* Assistant role
* Scope of responsibility
* Output format
* JSON response structure
* ATS evaluation rules
* Edge-case handling
* Safety constraints

This ensures consistent, structured, and reliable responses while maintaining a professional coaching experience.

# ⚙️ How It Works

1. Upload or paste your resume.
2. (Optional) Add a target Job Description.
3. Submit the resume for AI analysis.
4. Claude evaluates resume quality and ATS compatibility.
5. The application displays:

   * ATS Score
   * Resume Summary
   * Strengths
   * Risks
   * Keyword Gaps
   * Personalized Recommendations
6. Continue the session using the built-in AI chat for follow-up questions.
