# Day 14 - Job Red Flag Detector using Claude AI

## Objective

The goal of Day 14 was to use Claude AI to analyze a real-world job opportunity and identify potential risks before applying.

Instead of focusing only on salary, title, or company name, the task emphasized evaluating job descriptions critically and making data-driven career decisions.

---

## Task Performed

Using Claude AI, I analyzed a real Data Analyst opportunity and generated a complete risk assessment report.

The analysis included:

* Unrealistic Requirements Detection
* Toxic Workplace Signal Detection
* Remote Job Authenticity Check
* Hiring Risk Assessment
* Company Risk Evaluation
* Interview Question Generation

---

### Final Verdict

⚠️ Apply with Caution

---

## Major Red Flags Identified

* Compensation details not disclosed
* Internship duration not clearly specified
* Possible role scope ambiguity
* Limited company information available publicly
* Unclear full-time conversion pathway

---

## Positive Signals

* Fresher-friendly opportunity
* Relevant Data Analyst responsibilities
* Real business registration
* Power BI and analytics-focused role
* No unrealistic experience requirements

---

## Smart Interview Questions Generated

1. Is the internship paid? If yes, what is the stipend structure?
2. What is the internship duration?
3. Is there a PPO (Pre-Placement Offer) pathway?
4. What does a typical week look like for an intern?
5. What tools, datasets, and mentorship support will be provided?

---

## Key Learnings

* Every job opportunity should be evaluated before applying.
* Missing salary information can be an important warning sign.
* AI can assist in career decision-making beyond resume creation.
* Asking smart questions during interviews helps avoid future issues.
* Company transparency is an important factor when evaluating opportunities.
