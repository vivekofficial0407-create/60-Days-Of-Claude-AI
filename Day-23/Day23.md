# Day 23 – Build Customer & MVP Blueprint with Claude AI

## Objective

The objective of Day 23 was to convert a startup idea into a practical customer and MVP execution blueprint using Claude AI.

After validating the startup idea from a VC perspective in Day 22, this challenge focused on:

- Understanding customers
- Identifying pain points
- Defining MVP features
- Creating execution strategy

---

# Startup Idea

## AI-Powered Career Assistant Platform

An AI platform designed to help students and fresh graduates improve their career readiness.

The platform provides:

- Resume optimization
- ATS analysis
- Job description matching
- Skill gap identification
- LinkedIn improvement
- Personalized career roadmap

---

# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

Target Users:

- CS / IT / Data Science students
- Fresh graduates
- Entry-level tech professionals

Age:

18–25 years

Location:

Tier 1–3 Indian cities

Behavior:

- Uses LinkedIn
- Applies through job portals
- Learns through online resources
- Wants personalized career guidance

---

# Buyer Persona

Example User:

Rohit — 3rd year CS student

Profile:

- Has technical skills
- Built projects
- Applies for internships/jobs
- Does not understand ATS or recruiter expectations

Goals:

- Get first internship/job
- Improve resume
- Build recruiter visibility

Challenges:

- Low callbacks
- No career mentor
- Confusion about skills to learn

---

# Top Customer Pain Points

Major problems identified:

1. Resume not passing ATS filters

2. No feedback on application rejection

3. Confusion about next skills to learn

4. Weak LinkedIn presence

5. Difficulty understanding job descriptions

6. Expensive career coaching

7. Generic online advice

8. No personalized roadmap

---

# Customer Objections

Common objections:

## "Is this better than ChatGPT?"

Solution:

## "Too expensive"

Solution:

Free tier + affordable paid plans.


## "My college already helps"

Solution:

AI provides personalized 24/7 guidance.

# MVP Recommendation

- Resume upload
- AI resume score
- ATS keyword analysis
- Job description matching
- Skill gap report


## Avoid Initially:

- Mobile app
- Full LMS
- Mock interviews
