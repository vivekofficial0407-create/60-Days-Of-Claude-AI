## Key Learnings

1. AI responds better when instructions are specific.
2. Context and audience significantly improve output quality.
3. Output format requirements help AI organize information effectively.
4. Prompt Engineering is not about writing longer prompts.
5. Better prompts lead to more useful and actionable responses.

---

## My Biggest Takeaway

Prompt Engineering is not about talking more to AI.

It is about providing the right context, objective, audience, constraints, and structure so AI can generate exactly what you need.

The quality of AI output often depends on the quality of the instructions we provide.
