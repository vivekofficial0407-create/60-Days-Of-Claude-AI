❓ What is this?

The Personal AI Playbook is a personal, offline toolkit of reusable AI prompt systems, built specifically around how you — a CS/Data Science student and AI developer — actually use Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini for coding, documentation, content, and learning. Instead of hunting for the right prompt every time, you pick a ready-made workflow, fill in a few variables, and copy a polished prompt straight into your AI tool of choice.

  • Dashboard — overview of all your saved workflows and quick stats.
  • Coding, Documentation, Content, Research — categories of ready-made, editable prompt workflows tailored to your tasks.
  • Prompt Builder — assemble a custom prompt from labeled building blocks (role, objective, context, etc.) with a live preview.
  • Loop Builder — turn any one-shot prompt into a self-checking, autonomous "keep improving until done" prompt.
  • My Workflows — everything you've created, edited, duplicated, or favorited, saved locally in your browser (nothing leaves your device).
