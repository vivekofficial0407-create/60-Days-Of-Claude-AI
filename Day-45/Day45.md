# 🧠 AI Decision Strategist - Think Better. Decide Smarter.

> **Day 45 - 60 Days of Claude AI Challenge**

An AI-powered interactive decision support dashboard designed to help users make complex life and career decisions through structured reasoning instead of emotional bias. The application transforms a simple four-question interview into a comprehensive decision report featuring comparative analysis, cognitive bias detection, decision scoring, risk assessment, and an actionable execution plan.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

Making important decisions is often overwhelming because emotions, uncertainty, and cognitive biases influence our thinking.

**AI Decision Strategist** addresses this challenge by guiding users through a short interview and transforming their responses into a professional decision dashboard that visualizes trade-offs, evaluates alternatives, highlights hidden assumptions, and recommends the most suitable path.

Unlike generic AI chatbots, this application focuses on structured decision-making using proven strategic thinking frameworks presented through a modern, interactive interface.

---

# 🎯 Objective

The primary objective of this project is to:

- Improve decision-making using structured analysis
- Eliminate emotional bias during complex decisions
- Compare multiple options objectively
- Identify hidden assumptions and cognitive biases
- Visualize decision quality through scoring models
- Provide actionable recommendations instead of vague advice

# ⚙️ How It Works

1. User answers four structured decision questions.
2. AI evaluates the provided context.
3. Decision options are analyzed individually.
4. Hidden assumptions and biases are detected.
5. Every option receives weighted scores.
6. Risk scenarios are generated.
7. A seven-day validation plan is created.
8. The application recommends the strongest option with supporting reasoning.
