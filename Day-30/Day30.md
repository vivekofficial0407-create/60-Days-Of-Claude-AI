# Day 30 - Build Supply Chain Builder: An Interactive Supply Chain Strategy Simulator

## Project Overview

As part of the 60 Days of Claude AI Challenge, I built an interactive supply chain simulation application using Claude AI.

The project is called:

# Supply Chain Builder


The goal of this application is to teach beginners how real-world supply chains work through an interactive business strategy experience.

The user acts as a decision maker and designs a company's supply chain strategy by making operational choices.


---

# Objective

The simulator helps users understand how different supply chain decisions impact business performance.

It focuses on:

- Supplier management
- Manufacturing decisions
- Warehouse planning
- Transportation strategy
- Inventory optimization
- Business trade-offs


---

# Technology Used

- React
- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript
- Babel CDN
- Claude AI


The application is built as a single HTML file and runs directly in the browser.


---

# Key Features


## 1. Beginner-Friendly Introduction

The application starts by explaining:

What is a supply chain?

It helps users understand:

- Suppliers
- Factories
- Warehouses
- Transportation
- Customers


before making decisions.


---

# 2. Random Company Generation

Every simulation generates a fictional company profile.

Includes:

- Industry
- Products
- Countries served
- Demand level


This makes every playthrough unique.


---

# 3. Supply Chain Strategy Decisions

The user builds a complete supply chain by selecting:


## Supplier Strategy

Options:

- Single Supplier
- Multiple Suppliers


Impact:

- Cost
- Risk
- Reliability


---

## Factory Location

Users choose manufacturing locations.

Decision affects:

- Production cost
- Logistics
- Delivery speed


---

## Warehouse Strategy

Options:

- Centralized warehouse
- Multiple warehouses


Impact:

- Storage cost
- Delivery performance
- Customer satisfaction


---

## Transportation Method

Options:

- Road
- Rail
- Sea
- Air


Impact:

- Speed
- Cost
- Sustainability


---

## Inventory Strategy

Options:

- Low Inventory
- Balanced Inventory
- High Inventory


Impact:

- Risk
- Stock availability
- Working capital


---

# 4. Real-Time Business Metrics

After every decision, the simulator updates:

## Metrics:

- Cost
- Delivery Speed
- Risk
- Customer Satisfaction
- Sustainability


Animated progress bars show how decisions affect the business.


---

# 5. Final Supply Chain Dashboard

At the end, the application generates:

## Overall Supply Chain Score

(0-100)


Along with:

- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Biggest Risk
- Recommended Improvements
