# Day 16 – Build Your First Stock Research Skill

## Objective

The goal of Day 16 was to create a reusable AI-powered Stock Fundamental Research Skill in Claude and use it to analyze a listed company using fundamental analysis techniques.

---

## Skill Created

### Skill Name

stock-fundamental-research

### Description

Analyze Indian and global listed companies using fundamentals, financial statements, business quality, competitive advantages, valuation, risks, and growth prospects.

The skill was designed to:

* Analyze stocks using company fundamentals
* Study financial statements
* Review valuation metrics
* Evaluate growth potential
* Analyze ownership patterns
* Identify business risks
* Generate investor-friendly research reports

---

## Company Selected

### Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)
