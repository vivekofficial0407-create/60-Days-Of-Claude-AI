# Day 24 - Build a Startup Business Strategy & Investment Readiness Report using Claude AI

## Objective

The goal of Day 24 was to analyze a startup idea from a business and investment perspective using Claude AI.

After validating the startup idea and creating the Customer & MVP Blueprint, this challenge focused on:

- Business model
- Revenue strategy
- Go-to-market planning
- Customer acquisition
- Investment readiness

---

# Startup Overview

## AI-Powered Career Assistant Platform

An AI-powered platform helping students and fresh graduates improve their career opportunities through:

- AI Resume Analysis
- ATS Optimization
- Job Description Matching
- Skill Gap Identification
- Personalized Career Roadmaps

The target market is Indian engineering students and fresh graduates.
