# Day 31 – AI Supply Chain Control Tower (Simulation Report)

## 📌 Overview
This project simulated the role of **Head of Operations** in a global supply chain using an AI-powered Control Tower dashboard.  
The objective was to monitor live operational alerts, make rapid decisions, and optimize key business KPIs under time pressure.

---

## 🎯 Objective
- Manage real-time supply chain disruptions
- Prioritize and resolve operational alerts
- Maintain and improve business KPIs
- Maximize overall operational performance score

---

## 🧠 Experience Summary
During the simulation, multiple high-priority operational disruptions were handled such as:
- Port congestion
- Supplier delays
- Transport breakdowns
- Demand spikes
- Inventory mismatches
- Weather disruptions

Each decision impacted:
- Service Level
- Customer Satisfaction
- Inventory Health
- Transportation Efficiency
- Operating Cost
- Revenue Protection

The challenge required balancing speed, accuracy, and strategic thinking under increasing alert frequency.
